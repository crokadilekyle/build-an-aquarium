How To Play

  • Drive with WASD or the arrow keys.
  • Follow the glowing target ring to earn delivery money.
  • Pull into race gates and press E to start a timed race.
  • Race payout depends on placement. Faster tiers help, but good driving matters.
  • Open the menu any time to view your car, equip owned cars, or buy more.

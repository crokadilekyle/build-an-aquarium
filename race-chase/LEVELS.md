# Race Chase Level Reference

Source images: `race-chase/car-level-images/`

This file is the working source-of-truth for car tiers in the `race-chase` game. Some castings are easy to recognize, but a few are ambiguous from the top-down photos. For code and content work, use the stable IDs below first and treat the "looks like" text as a human-readable description.

## Common

- `common-white-offroad-pickup` - white off-road pickup with black hood stripe
- `common-blue-offroad-suv` - blue off-road SUV/truck
- `common-red-offroad-pickup` - red off-road pickup

## Rare

- `rare-light-blue-vintage-truck` - light blue vintage/custom truck
- `rare-black-suv` - black SUV
- `rare-red-muscle-car` - red muscle car
- `rare-silver-porsche-sedan` - silver Porsche-style sedan
- `rare-red-hatchback` - red hatchback/sport sedan

## Elite

- `elite-dark-gray-sedan` - dark gray sedan
- `elite-silver-lambo` - silver Lamborghini-style supercar
- `elite-blue-future-coupe` - blue futuristic coupe, looks like a BMW i8-style car
- `elite-gray-striped-muscle` - gray muscle car with dual black stripes

## Super Elite

- `super-elite-white-roadster` - white open roadster with exposed gold engine
- `super-elite-white-lambo` - white Lamborghini-style supercar with green-tinted windows
- `super-elite-red-classic-muscle` - red classic muscle car
- `super-elite-gold-turbo-racer` - gold vintage turbo racer

## Specialist

- `specialist-green-lambo` - bright green Lamborghini-style supercar
- `specialist-white-hypercar` - white hypercar with black center stripe

## OG

- `og-black-hypercar` - black hypercar mclaren

## Quick Tier Counts

- `Common`: 3
- `Rare`: 5
- `Elite`: 4
- `Super Elite`: 4

How It Works

Every level has its own drop target. Reach the finish line after landing that big drop and the next level opens up. Clear all levels in a world to unlock the next one.

Controls

Desktop: `A / D` or arrow keys to skate, `Space / W / Up` to jump, `R` to restart.

Mobile: use the on-screen buttons under the game.

Controls

Drop Into Royale Island

Press Play Royale Island to spawn in. Move with WASD or arrow keys, aim with the mouse, and click or press Space to fire. Walk over guns and health to pick them up.

How To Play

  • Move with `WASD` or arrow keys.
  • Aim with the mouse.
  • Click or press `Space` to shoot.
  • Walk over loot to grab guns and health.
  • Clear every AI skin to win the round.
